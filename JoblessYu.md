# How to Use JoblessYu 
## Commands

- `/jobs`: Open the interactive **Job Sweeper** search interface.

---

## Job Cards & Display Information

### Core Information (Always Displayed)
- **Title**: Job Position Name
- **Company**: Employer / Organization
- **Location**: City / Region (Ho Chi Minh, Ha Noi, All)
- **Overview**: Neutral 1-sentence AI summary of the job description
- **Apply Link(s)**: Direct multi-platform links to apply (e.g. Indeed, LinkedIn, ITViec)

### Optional Metadata (Displayed if Available)
- **Level**: `Intern`, `Fresher`, `Junior`, `Senior`
- **Job Type**: `Full-time`, `Part-time`, `Contract`
- **Salary**: Raw salary range (e.g. `$1,500 - $2,500/month` or `20 - 30 triệu`)
- **Remote**: `Yes` (if remote, hybrid, or work-from-home is offered)
- **Tags**: Tech stack pills grouped by category (Cloud, IaC, Pipeline, Containers, Security, Languages, Data/DB, AI)

---

## Privacy & Ephemeral Interactions

Both the **Job Sweeper filter interface** and all **job search result cards** are tagged as **Ephemeral (`"Only you can see this"`)**.
